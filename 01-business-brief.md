# Business Brief

## Company Overview

**Company:** Northstar Advisory Group  
**Industry:** Commercial Insurance Brokerage  
**Location:** New York City  
**Company Size:** ~50 employees  
**Active Clients:** ~600 commercial clients  
**Department:** Account Management & Operations  
**Process Under Review:** Client document intake and processing during annual commercial insurance renewals

---

## Business Context

Northstar Advisory Group is a growing commercial insurance brokerage serving approximately 600 business clients.

As clients approach their annual insurance renewals, they submit documents containing updated business, employee, property, claims, and insurance information. Northstar's account operations team reviews these documents before the account can move through the renewal process.

The current process relies heavily on manual employee work.

Account coordinators must identify and classify incoming documents, extract relevant information, compare new information against existing client records, identify missing or conflicting information, update internal systems, escalate unusual cases, and prepare follow-up communications for clients.

As Northstar's client base grows, the volume of documents requiring processing grows with it.

---

## Business Problem

Northstar's current renewal-document process does not scale efficiently with client growth.

Increasing renewal volume requires increasing amounts of employee time for repetitive document-processing work. This consumes operational capacity, increases labor costs, creates opportunities for manual error, and can result in inconsistent client response times.

Under the current operating model:

**More clients → More documents → More coordinator hours → More required headcount**

If the process remains unchanged, Northstar risks having its operational costs and staffing requirements grow alongside client volume, limiting the operational leverage gained from continued business growth.

## Business Impact

The current process creates four primary operational concerns:

### 1. Cost

Increasing document volume requires additional coordinator labor and may eventually require additional operations headcount.

### 2. Capacity

Employees spend substantial time on repetitive document processing rather than higher-value client and account work.

### 3. Quality

Manual classification, data extraction, comparison, and data entry introduce opportunities for errors and rework.

### 4. Customer Experience

Processing delays and inconsistent turnaround times can slow client communication during time-sensitive insurance renewals.

---

## Business Objective

Northstar wants to increase its renewal-processing capacity without proportionally increasing operations headcount.

Any redesigned process should:

- Reduce repetitive administrative work
- Improve processing speed
- Maintain or improve accuracy
- Reduce unnecessary rework
- Preserve appropriate human oversight
- Maintain data security and client confidentiality
- Support continued client growth without equivalent growth in operational workload

The objective is not to automate the process simply because automation or AI is available.

The objective is to determine whether the process can be redesigned to produce greater operational capacity, consistency, and efficiency while maintaining acceptable levels of risk and human oversight.

---

## Initial Project Scope

This case study will evaluate Northstar's commercial insurance renewal document-intake workflow from the point at which client documents are received through initial processing, internal system updates, exception handling, and preparation of client follow-up communication.

The project will begin with stakeholder discovery and current-state process analysis before determining where, if anywhere, AI or automation should be introduced.
